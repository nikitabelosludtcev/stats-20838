Thresholds the solutions actually use

  • p < 0.05 is the course’s main “significant”.
  • 0.05 ≤ p < 0.10 is still commented on in the mock: “significant at 10%”. Do not throw the row away.
  • p ≥ 0.10 is “no link is visible”. That is not a proof of a zero effect, and it is not permission to raise the price.
  • For an OR, the “no effect” interval contains 1. For an OLS coefficient and for a logit coefficient, “no effect” is 0.
  • Sample Box balance: the standardized difference after matching is below 0.10 on the X’s that are shown.

Five sentences worth learning by heart

  1. Not rejecting H₀ did not prove the claim exactly true.
  2. The SD is about how people differ. The SE is about how precise the mean is.
  3. A logit coefficient is not an addition to a probability. First exp, then percent of the odds.
  4. Who is at risk of leaving, and whom an offer will keep, are different questions.
  5. A fat gap between observed groups is not the effect of a letter, a coupon, or a box.
