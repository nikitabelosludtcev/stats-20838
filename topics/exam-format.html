Week 8 · assessment and the closing class

How the 75 minutes and the grade are built

Half of an attending student’s effort is the project, and half is the written exam. The exam does not ask you to run Python. It asks you to read someone else’s output and decide what the manager should do.

Attending, you did the team projectNon-attending
WeightProject 50% and written exam 50%Written exam 100%
The paperThe same: 75 minutes. 4 multiple choice and open questions on a short case. A correct MCQ = 1, a wrong one = 0
ContentSlides, cases, code, Lemon & Verhoef, Ascarza 2018, the Artea caseThe same, plus Dubé et al. 2025, Madrigal, and Blattberg chapters 20, 21, and 24. The questions on those texts are different
RoundingThe closing class’s example: a project of 30 and an exam of 25 give 0.5×30 + 0.5×25 = 27.5, and that is 28. 27.2 rounds to 27

The uploaded mock blank is structured like this: 4 MCQ, then two mini-cases. In the solution file the Green Product case is named as an extra part “for the maximum mark”. Prepare both: the main case about a 0/1 model, and a separate piece about a crooked field test. The folder gives only 2 sample MCQ, not all four.

The closing class repeats: statistical software is not run on the exam. The methods are there so you can read an output. The required papers must be read: the themes have already sounded in the course, and the paper goes one step deeper. If your project was only about forecasting and targeting, revise the field test separately, and the other way around. The exam covers every technique in the course, not only yours.

What a strong open answer contains

  1. The name of the model, and why it fits this Y.
  2. Which rows are significant at 5%, which only at 10%, and which are not. Say the threshold out loud.
  3. The translation: OLS in units of Y, logit in percent of the odds through (OR − 1) or (1 − OR).
  4. The dummy baseline is named: “relative to customers older than 55”, “relative to the control”.
  5. An interaction is not confused with the main effect.
  6. Cause is kept apart from a link. If groups were cut by city, or people signed themselves into a programme, you say selection bias.
  7. The recommendation comes after the caveat, not instead of it. “Do not raise the price only because the p-value is large” is exactly that move.

Peer review does not affect the exam grade. It is a voluntary letter about contribution to the project, on a 0–100 scale, with a penalty of up to 3 points off the project part in a rare case. Do not spend test-preparation time on it.

The order of these days. Day 1: this page and touchpoints. Days 2–3: the mock with the solutions closed, a 75-minute timer, written by hand or in a note. Day 4: open the solutions and check every sentence. Day 5: a second pass out loud in 60 minutes. Day 6: the formula sheet and the list of gaps, with no new topics.
