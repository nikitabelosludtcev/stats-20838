A trap in the mock tableOn the row Promo × Male the coefficient is 0.17, and the Exp(Coef.) column shows 1.49. exp(0.17) ≈ 1.19, not 1.49. The other ORs in the table agree with their coefficients. On your answer, compute exp yourself, and do not build a 49% from that cell. The solution in words leans on the sign and the p-value of the interaction, not on 1.49.
